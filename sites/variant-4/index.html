Methodology & FAQ
How are commissions calculated?
Allocated deals × eligible average funded amount × illustrative rate, or eligible deals × configured fixed fee. Monthly projections incorporate the selected production scenario, growth, capacity and ramp. Annualized earnings are month one × 12; modeled totals sum the individual months.
Are these actual Moonshine Capital compensation terms?
No. Rates, products, tier thresholds and scenario multipliers are hypothetical unless an approved compensation schedule is configured.
What is the difference between earnings and receipts?
Earned commissions follow modeled production. Receipts shift by your selected payment delay. Estimated pre-tax operating income is receipts less modeled expenses.
What about recurring income and bonuses?
These features are disabled until applicable compensation rules are supplied. They are not included in the default scenario.
