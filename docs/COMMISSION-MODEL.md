# Commission model

`shared/config/commission-config.js` is the central configuration, `shared/lib/calculator-engine.js` the pure engine. Demonstration rates are 3% for all five hypothetical categories, equally allocated. Baseline 10 × $50,000 × 3% = $15,000 per month, $180,000 annualized, $180,000 earned in 12 months, $540,000 earned in 36 months. No growth, bonus, recurring or expense assumptions by default.

Scenario multiplier adjusts modeled deals before capacity and ramp. Month m (zero-based) = min(capacity, input deals × scenario factor × (1 + growth/100)^m × ramp factor). Ramp factor increases linearly to 1 over the chosen months. Fractional deals mean expected production. Each product's allocation determines eligible deals. Funded amount basis is average deal size; percentage commissions use allocated deals × amount × rate. Fixed-fee compensation uses allocated deals × fixed fee and contributes zero to eligible funded-capital volume. Monthly product commissions are rounded to cents before summing. Monthly earnings include configured recurring and bonus amounts only when enabled. Recurring rules use one cohort per product (prior active cohort × retention + new eligible deals); bonuses require threshold qualification each month. A payment delay shifts receipts, not earnings. Expenses reduce receipt-based pre-tax operating income. Target calculation seeks the smallest integer input deal count satisfying month-one gross within configured limits.

Verified mode requires effective date and approved source; set product eligibility, methods, rates, and feature flags from an approved schedule. Verified rates are locked. This release supports funded-amount and fixed-fee methods only; contract-specific deduction/clawback, split, team override, alternate basis, and multi-rule recurring schedules require additional engine implementation and tests before enabling verified operation. Never publish private program agreements or credentials in a static build. The visible disclaimer is not legal approval.
