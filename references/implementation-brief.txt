### 11. Shared calculation engine

Create deterministic financial functions in:

`shared/lib/calculator-engine.js`

For percentage-based products:

Allocated funded deals = Total funded deals × Product allocation percentage.

Eligible funded volume = Allocated funded deals × Average eligible funded amount.

Gross product commission = Eligible funded volume × Applicable commission rate.

For fixed-fee products:

Gross product commission = Eligible funded deals × Fixed commission amount.

Total gross commission = Sum of deal-based commissions + Applicable recurring commissions + Qualified bonuses.

Annualized gross commission = Current monthly gross commission × 12.

Modeled first-year and 36-month commissions must equal the sum of their respective monthly projections.

If operating expenses are enabled, calculate estimated pre-tax operating income from modeled commission receipts minus modeled expenses.

Distinguish commissions earned, commission receipts, gross commission, and estimated pre-tax operating income.

Do not double-count recurring compensation, bonuses, or conversion rates.

Use the same calculation functions in every variation and in exported reports.
